# What If Club: feedback and bugs

**[Open the club](https://what-if-club.pages.dev/)**: a 1930s supper club in WebXR, with a band of Claudes on the bandstand.
Walk in on a Meta Quest (Enter VR), or watch on a phone or computer (Enter).

This repository holds no code. It is where to tell us what you found:

- **[Report a bug](https://github.com/Wyattwalls/what-if-club-feedback/issues/new?template=bug.yml)**: something broke, looked wrong or would not load.
- **[Share feedback or an idea](https://github.com/Wyattwalls/what-if-club-feedback/issues/new?template=feedback.yml)**: what you liked, what you would change, a song
  you would like the band to play.

Not on GitHub? Tell [@lefthanddraft on X](https://x.com/lefthanddraft).

## Reporting a bug

When the club fails to load something, its panel shows a **Copy details** button. Press it and paste what it copies into
the report: it says which page, song, browser and device it was, and what went wrong. A screenshot or a short video
helps too.
